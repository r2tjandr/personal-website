## Medical device design and development consulting

I support medical device start-ups across the whole product lifecycle, from first concept to market launch. I have taken a complex hardware, software and AI in vitro diagnostics (IVD) system from lab bench to commercial product, and I bring that experience to your program. IVD is a particular strength, but the approach applies to medical devices generally.

## Services by lifecycle phase

### 1. Concept, feasibility and user needs
Define the clinical problem, the intended use and the user needs. Test technical feasibility early, before you commit budget to development.

### 2. Design and development under design controls
Build a Design History File (DHF) that holds up to review: requirements, system architecture for hardware, software and AI components, and traceable design inputs and outputs. I work within ISO 13485 and 21 CFR 820 design controls.

### 3. Risk management, verification and validation
Plan risk management, then verification and validation, so the evidence matches your requirements and your regulatory path.

### 4. Preclinical and clinical evidence
Plan and run the studies that support your claims, from bench and animal work to clinical trials. I have taken a device through animal and clinical trials.

### 5. Regulatory strategy and submissions
Choose a pathway and prepare the submission dossier. I have taken a device to FDA 510(k) clearance, a Health Canada medical device licence and SFDA approval.

### 6. Quality management system (QMS)
Set up or improve a QMS that fits your stage: ISO 13485 and 21 CFR 820.

### 7. Design transfer, manufacturing and launch
Move from a working design to a manufacturable, scalable product, and prepare for commercial launch.

### 8. Intellectual property strategy
Map patent landscapes, run freedom-to-operate analyses and protect your innovations. I grew an IP portfolio 6x and helped secure $200K+ in non-dilutive funding.

## How I work

- **Scoped engagements.** A defined problem, a clear deliverable and an agreed timeline.
- **Fractional support.** Part-time technical and regulatory leadership for your team.
- **Gap assessments.** A review of your current design, quality or regulatory position, with a prioritized list of what to fix.
